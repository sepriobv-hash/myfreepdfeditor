Description:

MyFreePDFEditor helps you edit, sign, merge, compress and organize PDFs in your browser. Extract selected pages, recognize printed text from scans, or remove marked sensitive areas using a flattened redacted copy. Core tools work without an account or watermark, and PDF content is processed on your device. OCR supports English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish and Italian. Review the output before sharing; conversion and recognition quality depend on the source document.
